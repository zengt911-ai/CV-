About

Front of the room, behind the scenes

I'm a third-year Global Operations Management student at City University of Hong Kong, and most of my week happens somewhere between a classroom and a stage. I run journalism, media-literacy and STEM workshops for primary and secondary students, emcee competitions with hundreds of participants, and pick up the operational slack behind large university events.

The thread through all of it is making things run smoothly for other people — breaking a complicated idea into something a ten-year-old can actually enjoy, keeping a schedule on track when a microphone dies, or making sure a nervous participant backstage feels steady before they walk on. I studied operations, but I practise it in rooms full of people.
